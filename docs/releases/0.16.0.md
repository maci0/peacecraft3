# PeaceCraft III 0.16.0

Every shipped campaign map now starts. All 79 chapters in the campaign index,
the four credits maps and the 14 orc bonus sub-maps reach the end of their
startup scripts and unit placement against a local Legacy 1.29.2.9231 install
(97 of 97, up from 33 of 79 before this cycle). Credits maps start from the
Credits button and the sub-maps through the `ChangeLevel` route their parent
uses. Behaviour is decoded from the original executable and shipped data; parts
that are not decoded still refuse with a message naming what is missing.

## Natives and orders

- Waygates, `SetDestructableMaxLife`, `UnitSetUsesAltIcon`,
  `SetAltMinimapIcon`, `SetReservedLocalHeroButtons`,
  `SetCameraTargetController`, `SetCameraField`, `RecycleGuardPosition`,
  `SetUnitCreepGuard`, `AddUnitToStock` (shop slot defaults read from the
  executable), `AddUnitAnimationProperties`, `UnitIgnoreAlarm`,
  `UnitModifySkillPoints`, `Get/SetUnitUserData`, `UnitId`, `IsUnitIdType`,
  `GetPlayerUnitCount`, `SetUnitUseFood`, `Get/SetDestructableOccluderHeight`,
  `SetDestructableAnimationSpeed`, `SetUnitFlyHeight` on any unit,
  `GetGameTypeSelected`, `MultiboardSuppressDisplay` (stored), `I2S` and
  `DoNotSaveReplay`.
- Orders: autocast ON for the shared-handler classes, with group exclusion;
  `unroot`; delayed `burrow`; `defend`; Moon Well `rechargeon`; `sentinel`;
  `entangleinstant` gold-mine conversion; wisp (`Awha`) and ghoul (`Ahrl`)
  lumber harvest.
- The `rtma` upgrade effect changes the player's maximum allowed unit count.

## Units, items and world

- Footprint placement: the `BuildingAngle` and facing quarters, odd-sized
  footprints, the border clamp, footprint doodads in `SetDoodadAnimationRect`
  and `fatLOS` occluder footprints in the sight grid.
- Movement speed is split into base, bonus list and additive multiplier, so
  slow, frost and boots combine as the original does. Negative mana
  regeneration drains to zero.
- Held items: orbs of Fire, Frost, Corruption, Venom, Darkness and Lightning,
  Kil'jaeden's orb, Frostguard, several orbs on one holder, Pendant of Mana,
  Runed Bracers, Gloves of Haste, Boots of Speed, the vampiric attack, the Ankh
  of Reincarnation, the Gem of True Seeing (stored), the spell shield (admitted)
  and the Unholy, Devotion, Brilliance and Command auras on a decoded aura host.
- Purge (dispel and decaying slow), the Black Arrow mark and rooted Ancient
  weapons at creation.
- Map object data: custom items over a base row, undefined ability ids skipped
  at creation, and `w3b` destructable overrides.

## Tooling

- Startup probes for maps reached by `ChangeLevel` and by the Credits button.
- Re-notes for this cycle's decodes, including several still open: automatic
  casting, invisibility, saved games, multiboards and string natives.

Original assets remain local and are not included in the release or browser
demo. The README screenshots are fresh 0.16.0 captures; see
[capture details](../screenshots/README.md).

## Limits

Startup is the scope of the 97-map result. A first five-minute horizon run of
the Human campaign passed eight missions. RoC Human08 stopped at
`LeaderboardSetItemLabelColor`. The three interludes it reached failed the
probe's control-return check, which does not yet accept an interlude's map
exit. A locked-campaign launch gap stopped the run at the Night Elf campaign.

Not implemented: automatic casting of autocast abilities; invisibility and
detection; `SaveGame`/`LoadGame` and zone revisits; multiboards beyond the
suppress flag; most string natives (`R2S` and others); spell shield blocking;
Purge's pause and summoned-unit damage; the Black Arrow summon; timed Entangle;
the Town Bell militia conversion; cargo load; Mana Shield and Immolation ON.
The 0.15.0 menu limits still apply; see [0.15.0](0.15.0.md) and
[menu acceptance](../menu-acceptance.md).

  and `pcx-net/session-server`: 6,030 passed, zero failures, 373
